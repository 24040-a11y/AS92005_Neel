FAQ How much insects means its infested? What pests are considered harmful? What pest should you focus on getting rid of first how can i know if a pest is invasive?

There isn‘t a specific number of pests to be infested by it simply means that they have a established breeding sites and cause a persistent problems

All Pests are considered harmful but to different things. Mosquitos, bed bugs, cockroaches, and rodents are harmful to humans whereas termites and rodents are harmful to the house.

You should try get rid of every pest but number one priority should be rodents like rats and mice due to their rapid breeding rates and health risks because of contamination.

To find out if its invasive, find out if its native to your area by searching up, consulting regional data bases or asking the neighborhood
