To keep pests out before they even come in seal up cracks and crevices: Pests can enter through small openings. inspect the exterior and interior of your home.

Cleanup crops: clean up any fallen fruits or vegtable if your grow fruits or veggies.

remove standing water: some insect pests breed in stagnant water. get rid of any standing water around your property.

use insect repellent: when your outside, use insect repellent to keep yourself safe from insect bites

store food scraps and rubbish properly:Food scraps in compost and rubbish bin can attract many pests to it.

treat pets for fleas: most pets carry fleas so its important to get them treated for it

vacuum regularly: vacuuming can help remove pests and their eggs from your furniture and carpets

seal food containers: ensure that all food containers are tightly sealed so pests can go into them
wash sheets regularly: wash sheets and bedding regularly to get rid of bed bugs
